How to Use

Enter your current cruise altitude
This is the altitude you're flying at right now, e.g. FL350 = 35,000 ft.
Enter your target altitude
Usually the airport field elevation + pattern altitude, or the initial approach fix (IAF) altitude. Common: 3,000 ft for most airports.
Set your ground speed
Check your current GS in the flight simulator. This affects required vertical speed.
Note the TOD distance
When you're TOD Distance NM from your destination, begin descending at the calculated vertical speed.
Set V/S in flight simulator.
Use Autopilot → V/S mode and enter the calculated feet-per-minute value (as a negative number to descend).

Formula Reference

Top of Descent Distance
TOD (NM) = Altitude to lose (ft) ÷ 1,000 × NM per 1,000 ft
Vertical Speed
VS (fpm) = Ground Speed (kts) × 101.27 × tan(descent angle°)
V/S → Descent Angle
Angle (°) = atan( VS (fpm) ÷ (GS (kts) × 101.27) ) × 180 ÷ π
V/S → TOD Distance
TOD (NM) = GS (kts) × (Alt to lose ÷ VS) ÷ 60
Quick Rule (3° / 3:1)
TOD ≈ Alt to lose ÷ 1,000 × 3 NM  |  VS ≈ GS × 5
